Generative AI is a branch of artificial intelligence that creates new content such as text, images, audio and code.

Large Language Models are AI models trained on large amounts of text data.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines information retrieval with language generation.

Embeddings convert text into numerical vectors.

Vector databases store embeddings and perform similarity search.

LangChain is a framework used to develop applications with language models.

ChromaDB is a vector database used to store and retrieve embeddings.
